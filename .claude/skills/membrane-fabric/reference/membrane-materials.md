# Membrane materials: data and behaviour

Tags: [V] = confirmed from a named source via search excerpt; [U] = industry typical or
background knowledge, not verified; confirm with datasheets.

## 1. PVC-coated polyester (PES/PVC)

Woven polyester base cloth (1100 dtex for light grades, 1670 dtex and up for heavy grades [U]), coated both sides with
plasticised PVC plus a topcoat (acrylic < weldable PVDF < PVDF/acrylic < PVF film/Tedlar
laminate). Welding: HF (RF, 27.12 MHz), hot-air or hot-wedge. High-PVDF lacquers are
ground off at the seam [U]. Foldable, so suitable for demountable and retractable roofs.

Datasheets [V]:
* Serge Ferrari Tenseo Advanced 1002 S2 (Précontraint): 1050 g/m², 420/400 daN/5cm = 84/80 kN/m, tear 55/50 daN, 0.78 mm, roll 267 cm.
* Mehler Valmex FR 1000 Mehatop (Type III): 1050 g/m², 6000/5500 N/5cm = 120/110 kN/m, tear 900/800 N, roll 250 cm (300 on request).
* Verseidag Duraskin B4915 (Type III, 1100 g/m²): roll 250 cm.

Other properties: elongation at break 15–25 % [U]; stretch under prestress 1–3 % (vs < 0.5 % for PTFE) [V];
fire B-s2,d0 / B-s1,d0 (EN 13501-1) [V], NFPA 701 / ASTM E84 [V]; life 15–25 years with a PVDF/PVF topcoat [U].

## 2. PTFE-coated glass fibre

Woven E-glass (beta-glass) filament fabric with sintered PTFE coating, often an FEP top layer for sealing.
Stiff, low creep, self-cleaning; bleaches white under UV (initially beige) [V].
Range 84/80 to 160/140 kN/m at 800–1550 g/m² [V].

Service temperature −60…+260 °C (Chukoh) [V]; light transmission 12–17 % typical [V]; fire A2-s1,d0 and ASTM E136
non-combustible base [V]; life 25–35+ years [V]. Heat-sealed at about 350–385 °C with FEP film, seam efficiency 70–80 % [V].
**Do not fold sharply.** Roll on large cores and pad the folds. Water can temporarily reduce strength by up to about 20 % [V-search].
PTFE panels are often seamed at 1.5–2.5 m widths (narrower than the roll) to limit curvature error [V, Architen].

## 3. Silicone-coated glass
80–140 kN/m (4000–7000 N/5cm) [V]; light transmission 20–30 %, up to 40 % [V]; Euroclass A2 or B [V]; life 20–30 years [V].
Jointing by vulcanising, bonding or sewing (not weldable like PVC) [U]; soils more than PTFE unless topcoated [U].

## 4. ETFE foil
Film 50–300 µm (100–250 µm common) [U]; density 1.70–1.75 g/cm³ [V] (200 µm ≈ 350 g/m²); UTS 35–53 MPa,
yield 20–30 MPa, elongation 300–450 % [V]; below first yield E ≈ 660 MPa, ν ≈ 0.42 [V]; linear viscoelastic
limit about 1.5 MPa [V]; transmission 88–95 % [V]; B-s1,d0 [V]. Cushions 2–5 layers, 200–400 Pa, raised to
600–800 Pa under snow or wind [U]. Design recommendations: TensiNet Appendix A5 (2013) [V]. Nowoflon ET 6235 Z roll 1550 mm [V].

## 5. ePTFE fabric (Sefar Tenara 4T40HF)
1080 g/m², 4000/4000 N/5cm = 80/80 kN/m, transmission 38 %, reflection 59 % [V]. Fully and repeatedly foldable,
no plasticiser, UV-stable [V], so suited to retractable roofs.

## 6. Mesh and shade cloth
PVC-coated PES mesh: 5–50 % open area, reduced wind, not waterproof [U]. PTFE/glass open mesh exists for façades [V].
HDPE knitted shade cloth: 180–340 g/m², 50–95 % shade factor, 8–15 year life, sewn with PTFE thread, near-isotropic, high elongation [U].

| PES/PVC (design practice) | 500–1200 | 300–900 | 0.2–0.6 | 10–50 [U] |

PVC moduli rise to about 600/620 MPa after cyclic loading [V]. Fitted constants depend strongly on the test
protocol and on which ratios are included (Gosling/Bridgens, COST TU1303) [V]. Choose the set matching the governing
load state (prestress 1:1, snow, wind).

## 8. Compensation (pattern shrinkage)
From biaxial tests at design prestress (EN 17117-2:2021). Example 1.2 % warp / 2.5 % weft [V, low-authority blog].
Typical PES/PVC 0.5–1.5 % warp, 1–3.5 % weft; glass/PTFE 0–0.5 % warp, 1–4 % weft (large weft crimp) [U].
Batch-specific: test the delivered rolls. Apply decompensation at boundaries of fixed length (clamp lines,
rigid frames, cable pockets with fixed cable length).

## 9. Welded seams (see design criteria for strength use)
PVC HF about 140 °C, 80–90 % efficiency, 40–60 mm wide [V] (50–80 mm for Types IV–V [U]). Research: a 27 mm weld reached
87.8/75.8 N/mm; a 70 mm multilayer patch reached 72 kN/m (≈ 0.7 of substrate) [V].
PTFE heat-seal about 380 °C, 70–80 % efficiency, 50–75 mm wide (75–100 for high grades [U]).
